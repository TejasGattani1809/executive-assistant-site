Privacy policy

Executive Assistant · last updated 11 November 2026

Who uses this app

Executive Assistant is a personal app. It is run by its owner, for the owner only. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses

Only with the owner's permission, it uses the owner's Google Calendar: to see the owner's calendars and events, and to add, move or delete events when the owner asks. This is used only to show the owner's schedule, send the owner reminders, and create the events the owner requests.

Only with the owner's permission, it also reads the owner's Gmail, with read-only access: it can never send, delete or change an email. This is used only to show the owner a summary of their important emails, answer the owner's questions about their email, and turn an email into a to-do when the owner asks.

Where data is kept

Data is kept on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google account. The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with others, or used for advertising. To understand the owner's requests, message text and related details (such as event names, and email senders and subjects) are processed by an AI service (Google Gemini). The full text of an email is sent to the AI only when the owner asks about that email. One-time codes and passwords are removed before anything is sent to the AI.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
