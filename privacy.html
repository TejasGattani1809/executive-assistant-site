Privacy policy

Executive Assistant · last updated 5 November 2026

Who uses this app

Executive Assistant is a personal app. It is run by its owner, for the owner only. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses

Only with the owner's permission, it uses the owner's Google Calendar: to see the owner's calendars and events, and to add, move or delete events when the owner asks. This is used only to show the owner's schedule, send the owner reminders, and create the events the owner requests.

Where data is kept

Data is kept on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google account. The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with others, or used for advertising. To understand the owner's requests, message text and related details (such as event names) are processed by an AI service (Google Gemini).

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
