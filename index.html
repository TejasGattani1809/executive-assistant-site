Executive Assistant

A personal assistant, used through a private Telegram bot.

Executive Assistant helps its owner organise the day. It keeps a to-do list with reminders, remembers facts the owner chooses to save, sends a morning briefing and an evening wrap-up, can show and add events in the owner's own Google Calendar, and can show a summary of the owner's important Gmail (read-only).

It is a personal project, used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
